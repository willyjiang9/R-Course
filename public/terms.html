3. Posting reviews

  • Only post about your own genuine experience in a course.
  • Keep it honest and respectful. No harassment, hate speech, threats, or discrimination.
  • No personal information (phone numbers, addresses, private details) about anyone.
  • Stick to the course and teaching. Do not make accusations about a person's private life or unverified claims of illegal conduct.
  • Do not post spam, ads, or content you do not have the right to share.

By posting, you confirm the review is your own, and you give R'Courses a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display it on this site. You are responsible for what you post. We may remove any review at any time, for any reason, without notice.

4. Report or remove content

If you believe a review is inaccurate, abusive, infringing, or should be removed, email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with the course code, a description of the review, and the reason. We review requests promptly and will remove content that violates these terms.

5. Privacy

  • There are no accounts and we do not ask for your name, email, or UCR ID.
  • Reviews are posted anonymously. Do not include identifying details in your review text.
  • Reviews are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). The site is hosted on Vercel, which may collect standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operations.
  • Your browser's local storage is used to remember that you saw the welcome message and to limit repeat review submissions. It does not identify you.
